Welcome to the Flash Card Creator

Here you can create flashcards for any subject you want. Follow the steps below:

  1. Enter the name of your Flashcard.
  2. Enter the subject of your Flashcard.
  3. Enter the words for your Flashcard. Flashcards have to be 10 cards long. 5 are selected at a time
  4. Upload images for your Flashcard. They have to be images in .png or .jpg format. Remember to only use images that you have the right to use.
